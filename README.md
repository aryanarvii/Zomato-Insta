
# 🍴 TasteTube — Zomato Meets Instagram Reels  

TasteTube is a full-stack food discovery platform where users explore food items through **short video reels**, just like Instagram Reels.  
It allows users to **like, save, comment**, and visit partner restaurants directly.  
Food partners can upload their food videos, manage listings, and engage with customers in a visually rich way.

## 🧩 Features

### 👨‍🍳 For Users
- 🎥 Explore food through **short reels**
- ❤️ Like, 💾 Save, 💬 Comment on dishes  
- 🏪 Visit restaurant profiles & browse menus  
- 🧠 Smart recommendations (coming soon)  
- 🤖 Chatbot for personalized food & mood suggestions (planned)

### 🍕 For Food Partners
- 📤 Upload and manage food reels
- 🧠 Smart chatbot based analytics (coming soon) 
- 🧾 Track likes, saves & engagement 
- 📈 Showcase menu & offers  
- 🔐 Secure authentication & dashboard access

## 🏗️ Tech Stack

### Frontend
- ⚛️ **React.js (Vite)**
- 🎨 **TailwindCSS**
- 🍞 **React Toastify** for alerts
- 🔐 **Axios + Context API** for authentication

### Backend
- 🧠 **Node.js + Express.js**
- 🗄️ **MongoDB (Mongoose)**
- 🔑 **JWT Authentication + Cookies**
- 🖼️ **ImageKit** for media storage
- ⚙️ **Render** for deployment
